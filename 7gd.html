Memory tricks

Crowded to Clear.

Three big ideas

  1. Diffusion is particles spreading and mixing, all by themselves.
  2. It goes from high concentration to low concentration.
  3. It is fastest in gases, slower in liquids, slowest in solids.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is diffusion?

When particles spread and mix with each other, without anything moving them.

Which way does diffusion go?

From high concentration to low. Crowded to Clear!

Is diffusion faster in a gas or a liquid?

A gas! Gases Gallop, Liquids Linger, Solids Snail.

Does diffusion need wind?

No! No push, no blow.

How does diffusion help your body?

Food particles diffuse from the small intestine into your blood.
