Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

stimulants

StimULants

depressants

DePressAnts

Other drugs make impulses travel more slowly. These are called depressants.

Memory tricks

Stimulants Speed things up.

Three big ideas

  1. A drug changes the way your body works. Medicines are drugs too.
  2. All drugs can have side-effects. Some are addictive.
  3. Stimulants speed up impulses. Depressants slow them down.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a drug?

A substance that affects the way your body works.

Which organ breaks drugs down?

The liver.

What does a stimulant do to impulses?

It makes them travel faster. Stimulants Speed.

Name a depressant.

Alcohol, heroin, or solvents. Depressants Drag.

Why do some athletes take steroids?

To make their muscles grow bigger.
