Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

newton

NewTon

contact

ConTact

gravity

GravITy

magnetism

MagNetIsm

Memory tricks

Shape. Speed. Steer.
Touching Gate, Distance Gate.
Times ten for the newtons.
Mass stays. Weight wanders.

Three big ideas

  1. A force is a push or a pull. We measure it in newtons.
  2. Some forces touch. Some forces work from a distance.
  3. Mass is the amount of stuff. Weight is the pull of gravity.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Name three things a force can change.

Shape, speed and direction. Shape, speed, steer!

Name one contact force, and one non-contact force.

Friction is a contact force. Gravity is a non-contact force.

What are the units for weight, and for mass?

Weight is in newtons. Mass is in grams or kilograms.

A bag has a mass of three kilograms. What is its weight?

Thirty newtons! Times ten for the newtons.
