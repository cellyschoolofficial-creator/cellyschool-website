Privacy

Celly School is made for children, so this website collects as little as possible.

  • No accounts and no sign-up. The only personal details we collect are the ones a grown-up chooses to give for the game reports below: their email and their children’s first names.
  • No advertising cookies on this website.
  • The “Easier reading” setting is saved only in your own browser, on your device. It is never sent to us.
  • We count which pages are visited and which buttons are used (for example “download the workbook”, “play the video” or “finished a game”) with Umami. Umami uses no cookies and collects no personal information; we only see totals.
  • To make the lessons and games easier to use, we use three tools that look at how the website is used: Microsoft Clarity (clicks, scrolling and where people get stuck), PostHog (which pages and game rooms are used and where people stop; its data is kept in the EU) and Google Analytics (which websites and searches bring visitors). They hide anything typed and do not record names or contact details. Google Analytics' advertising and Google-signals features are switched off, and for visitors in the UK and Europe Clarity and Google Analytics do not set cookies. The privacy statements of Microsoft, PostHog and Google apply to their tools. You can switch these tools off on your device here:
  • We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, does not collect personal information and does not follow you to other websites. It only tells us totals, such as how many people visited a page and which country or website they came from.
  • Game reports. Talk to Celly games can email a grown-up a short report after each game. This is optional. To use it, a grown-up enters their email and, if they wish, their children’s first names (or nicknames), and ticks a box to say they are the parent or carer and agree to us keeping these details. That tick is their consent. We keep the email address and the first names in a private list in our Google account, together with each child’s games, answers, scores and progress over time, so we can send reports, recap results and the next recap for the right child, and use the anonymous totals to make the games and lessons better. The game results (which answers were right first time, the time taken and the words to practise) are sent to our Google account, which makes the PDF report and emails it. We never share or sell these details, and we do not send newsletters unless you ask for them. You can change your mind at any time: to see, change or delete your details, reply to any report email or write to cellyschool.official@gmail.com, and we will delete them. The device also remembers the email and names so a child can simply tap their name next time; “Remove from this device” on the Talk to Celly page clears them.
  • Lesson videos are hosted on YouTube. A video only loads from YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) when you press play. YouTube's own privacy policy then applies.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
