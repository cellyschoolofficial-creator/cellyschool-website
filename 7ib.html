Three big ideas

  1. Energy is kept in stores, like chemical, kinetic and gravitational.
  2. Energy moves between stores by heating, light, sound, electricity and forces.
  3. Energy is never made or destroyed. It only moves.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Name the six energy stores.

Chemical, kinetic, thermal, strain, gravitational, nuclear. Cats Keep Throwing Socks Great Nights!

Name the five ways energy is transferred.

Heating, light, sound, electricity, forces. Happy Lions Sing Every Friday!

What does the law of conservation of energy say?

Energy can't be created or destroyed. Never made. Never lost. Only moved.

What does a stretched bungee cord store?

Strain energy!
