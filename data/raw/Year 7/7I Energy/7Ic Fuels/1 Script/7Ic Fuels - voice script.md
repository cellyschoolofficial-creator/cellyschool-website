# 7Ic Fuels — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Host:** Volty the spark (physics hero), with Bolt as the sidekick. Volty's voice isn't chosen yet (see 7Ia).
- ⚑ **The peanut-oil engine** (true story, Ic10): this goes beyond the book ◇. At the 1900 Paris World's Fair, a diesel engine was shown running on peanut oil. The demonstration was arranged by the French government and run by a company building Diesel's engines, not by Rudolf Diesel himself. The script says "a diesel engine ran on peanut oil, and Diesel later wrote about it". It does not quote him.
- ⚑ **Coal from plants:** the book says coal formed from plants buried in mud, squashed by more layers, with heat from inside the Earth. The swamp forests with giant dragonflies are ◇; the script says "long ago" and "many millions of years", with no exact dates.
- ⚑ **"Coal is made of dinosaurs"** is a common myth. Bolt says it, and Volty corrects it: coal is mostly ancient plants, and oil and gas come from tiny sea creatures and plants.
- **Book facts:** a fuel stores chemical or nuclear energy that can easily be transferred. Burning a fuel doesn't make energy, it transfers it. Uranium releases energy a different way. 1 kg of nuclear fuel stores about 3 500 000 times as much energy as 1 kg of coal. Fossil fuels are non-renewable. Scientists aren't certain how long each will last (graph D). Biofuels are renewable; some are made from waste cooking oil. Wastes can be turned into methane. Most hydrogen today is made from natural gas, and a fuel cell car combines hydrogen with oxygen to make electricity.
- ⚑ **Dried dung as a fuel:** this comes from the creative design doc's book notes. The OCR copy of p141 is cut at that point, so check the page.
- **STEM page:** transport managers, best routes, computer models (GPS), one big truck vs four small vans, primary vs secondary data (book p142–143).
- ◇ **"Cows Often Graze"** (coal, oil, gas), **"Coal from land plants, oil from the ocean"**, **"A fuel is a store. Electricity is a road."** and **"Burning moves it, doesn't make it"** are Volty-made tricks.
- **Home lab:** the fossil sandwich (bread, jam, leaves). It's not for eating afterwards. Wash hands.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Ic Fuels (p140–141) and 7Ic Transporting goods (STEM, p142–143). Celly School's third lesson of 7I Energy.
- **Size:** 152 spoken lines (Volty 137, Bolt 15), 30 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1590 words. That is roughly 19–20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Volty the spark (voice ⚑ to choose).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** behind Joule Park is the Fuel Depot. Its barrels and coal bunkers are giant sealed Chemical jars. A big sign says "Electricity is NOT a fuel. It's a pathway." In Part two, the park's delivery trucks bring snacks to the stalls, and a transport manager plans their routes.
- **Case:** "The case of the sea creature in the fuel tank" (the 7Ib teaser).
- **Answered at the end:** oil formed from tiny animals and plants that lived in the sea millions of years ago. They died, sank, and were buried in mud and sand. More layers squashed them into crude oil. Petrol is made from oil. Their energy first came from the Sun.

**Main tricks:**
- "Cows Often Graze." Coal, Oil, Gas. (chew like a cow, three chews)
- "Coal from land plants. Oil from the ocean." (hand flat for land / hand waving for sea)
- "A fuel is a store. Electricity is a road." (cup your hands / draw a road with one finger)
- "Burning moves it, doesn't make it."
