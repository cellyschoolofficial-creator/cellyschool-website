# 7Ie Using resources — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Host:** Volty the spark (physics hero), with Bolt as the sidekick. Volty's voice isn't chosen yet (see 7Ia).
- ⚑ **Eunice Foote** (true story, Ie10): this goes beyond the book ◇. In 1856 she put thermometers in glass cylinders of different gases and left them in the sun. The carbon dioxide cylinder got hottest and stayed hot longest. She wrote that more of this gas would make the Earth warmer. Her paper was read at a science meeting in 1856, three years before John Tyndall's better-known experiments (1859). The script says "for a long time, few people knew her name" and doesn't say she was "forgotten for 150 years".
- ⚑ **Climate change:** the book says "most scientists who study the climate think" fossil fuels help make the Earth warmer, because burning them makes carbon dioxide. The script follows that wording and stays calm. There are no scary images or disaster claims.
- ⚑ **Wind farms and birds:** the book says some people object to wind farms because "they say they kill a lot of birds". The script keeps "some people say" and gives no numbers.
- ⚑ **Over 75 percent:** the book says over 75 percent of "our" electricity is generated using fossil fuels. The figure depends on country and year, so the script says "in the book's figures".
- **Book facts:**
  - Bulb A gives 18 J of light from 20 J, and bulb B gives 4 J, so A is more efficient.
  - The ride motor transfers energy to the carriages' gravitational store (useful) and to the motor and surroundings (wasted, thermal).
  - An efficient machine means less fossil fuel is burnt and electricity bills are cheaper.
  - Ways to use less fossil fuel: wear a jumper, walk, cycle or use public transport, use efficient appliances (labels show efficiency), and insulate homes.
- ◇ **Power and cost:** watts and the cost of electricity are not in this book (they come in 8K), so they are not taught.
- ◇ **"Waste Warms the World around"**, **"Jumper, Journey, Jacket for the house"** and **"Useful out, from total in"** are Volty-made tricks. Efficiency is taught as "18 out of 20 tokens", with no percentage formula.
- **Home lab:** keep the cocoa warm. A grown-up pours warm tap water, not from the kettle. Never touch a lit bulb.
- **Unit finale (Ie12):** a short recap of 7Ia–7Ie through Joule Park, and the 7J teaser.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Ie Using resources (p146–147) and 7Ie Making changes (Think Bigger, p148). Celly School's last lesson of 7I Energy, with the unit finale.
- **Size:** 154 spoken lines (Volty 140, Bolt 14), 31 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1610 words. That is roughly 19–20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Volty the spark (voice ⚑ to choose).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** the Efficiency Fountain. Every Joule Park machine has a fountain of tokens pouring in at the top. Useful tokens flow into a gold bucket. Wasted tokens spill over the edge into the Warm Fog. A dial shows how many reach the bucket.
- **Case:** "The case of the dim bulb" (the 7Id teaser: "two bulbs get the same energy. One glows bright, one barely glows. Why?").
- **Answered at the end:** both bulbs get 20 joules every second. Bulb A sends 18 out as light. Bulb B sends only 4 out as light; the other 16 heat the surroundings. Bulb B is less efficient. The energy isn't destroyed. It's wasted, spread out as heat.

**Main tricks:**
- "Waste Warms the World around." Wasted energy usually ends up heating the surroundings. (spread your hands out wide)
- "Jumper, Journey, Jacket for the house." Three ways to use less fuel: wear a jumper, walk or cycle the journey, insulate the house. (pull on a jumper / walk on the spot / hug a house)
- "Useful out, from total in." Efficiency: how many tokens reach the bucket out of all that went in.
