# 7Ib Energy transfers and stores — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Host:** Volty the spark (physics hero), with Bolt as the sidekick. Volty's voice isn't chosen yet (see 7Ia).
- ⚑ **James Joule** (true story, Ib10): this goes beyond the book ◇, though the book names the unit. He came from a brewing family near Manchester. In the 1840s a falling weight turned a paddle wheel in water, and the water warmed by a tiny amount. The honeymoon waterfall story (1847) comes mainly from William Thomson's (Lord Kelvin's) later account, so the script says "the story goes".
- ⚑ **Roller-coaster hook:** "the second hill is never taller than the first" is a standard physics point ◇, not a book sentence. The book shows the carriage storing gravitational potential energy at the top of the track (book D). The script says "can't climb higher than where it started" and blames friction and sound for the lost tokens.
- ⚑ **Sound and light are pathways, not stores.** The script never says "sound energy is stored". It follows the book's list of five transfers.
- **Book stores:** chemical, kinetic, thermal, strain (elastic potential), gravitational potential, nuclear. Nuclear energy can't be transferred by burning, and the Sun has a store of nuclear energy (book).
- **Book transfers:** heating, light, sound, electricity, forces. The law of conservation of energy: energy can't be created or destroyed, only transferred and stored in different ways. 2000 J goes into an electric fire each second, so 2000 J comes out (Q).
- ◇ **"Cats Keep Throwing Socks Great Nights"** (stores) and **"Happy Lions Sing Every Friday"** (transfers) are from the kit; **"Never made. Never lost. Only moved."** is a Volty-made trick.
- ◇ **Model crack:** the golden tokens are not real. Energy is a number we keep track of, like points in a game. The script says this once.
- **Home lab:** a bouncy ball. The comeback tub is a bonus: a plastic tub only, and a grown-up makes the holes.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Ib Energy transfers and stores (p138–139). Celly School's second lesson of 7I Energy.
- **Size:** 153 spoken lines (Volty 139, Bolt 14), 31 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1600 words. That is roughly 19–20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Volty the spark (voice ⚑ to choose).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** Joule Park's control room has the Jar Wall. Six labelled jars light up as rides run: Chemical, Kinetic, Thermal, Strain, Gravitational and Nuclear. Five glowing pipes join them: heating (orange), light (yellow), sound (green), electricity (blue) and forces (red). The Warm Fog in the sky collects tokens that spread out. The Counting Clock at the gate shows the park's total, and it never changes.
- **Case:** "The case of the roller coaster with no engine" (the 7Ia teaser).
- **Answered at the end:** at the top of the first hill, the car's gravitational jar is full. On the way down, forces move the tokens into its kinetic jar, and back up again. A few tokens leak into the Warm Fog every time, by heating and sound. So each hill must be lower than the first.

**Main tricks:**
- Stores: "Cats Keep Throwing Socks Great Nights." Chemical, Kinetic, Thermal, Strain, Gravitational, Nuclear. (pat tummy / run hands / fan face / stretch a band / point up high / tiny fist)
- Transfers: "Happy Lions Sing Every Friday." Heating, Light, Sound, Electricity, Forces.
- "Never made. Never lost. Only moved." (three claps)
