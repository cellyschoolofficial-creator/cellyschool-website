Organs: key facts

What is an organ?

An organ is a large part of a plant or animal that does an important job. Organs are made of different tissues working together. The job of an organ is called its function.

What do the organs in my body do?

brain – controls the body
skin – protects you and lets you sense things (the biggest organ)
heart – pumps blood
windpipe (trachea) – takes air to and from the lungs
lungs – get oxygen into the blood and get rid of carbon dioxide
diaphragm – a sheet of muscle that helps you breathe
gullet (oesophagus) – takes food from the mouth to the stomach
stomach – breaks up food
small intestine – breaks up food and absorbs it into the blood
large intestine – takes water out of food the body does not need
liver – makes, breaks down and stores substances
kidneys – clean the blood and make urine
bladder – stores urine

Good Snacks Stay Long: gullet, stomach, small intestine, large intestine.

What are the organs of a plant?

Real Snails Love Flowers, from the bottom to the top:

root – holds the plant in place and takes in water from the soil
stem – carries substances and holds up the leaves and flowers
leaf – traps sunlight to make food
flower – for reproduction; it makes seeds

What is photosynthesis?

Photosynthesis means making food using light. It happens in the leaves. The leaves need light, carbon dioxide from the air, and water. Plants in the shade often have big leaves to catch more light.

What is a storage organ?

A part of a plant that stores food for later, like a carrot or a potato.

What is a diagnosis?

A doctor's conclusion about what is wrong. Doctors test ideas and use the results as evidence, just like scientists.

Three big ideas

1. Every organ has a function.
2. Plants have organs too.
3. Leaves make food by photosynthesis, using light.
