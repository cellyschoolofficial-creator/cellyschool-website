CELLS AND MICROSCOPES – KEY FACTS  (new section for www.cellyschool.com/7ad, below "What you will learn")

What is a cell?
A cell is the basic unit of life. Every living thing is made of cells, like bricks in a house.

What are the parts of a light microscope?
- Eyepiece lens: at the top. You look through it.
- Objective lens: near the bottom, closest to the specimen.
- Stage: the flat table. The slide sits on it.
- Focusing wheel: makes the picture clear.
- Light (or mirror): shines up through the specimen.

How do you use a light microscope? (6 steps)
1. Put the smallest objective lens over the hole.
2. Turn the wheel, so the lens is close to the stage.
3. Put the slide on the stage.
4. Turn on the light, or move the mirror.
5. Look into the eyepiece.
6. Turn the wheel slowly, until the picture is clear.
Safety: never point the mirror at the Sun. It can hurt your eyes.

Why must a specimen be thin?
So light can get through it. A thin coverslip keeps the specimen flat and stops it drying out. A stain (a coloured dye) helps us see the parts of the cell.

How do you work out magnification?
Multiply the two lenses: eyepiece × objective.
Eyepiece ×10 and objective ×40 gives ×400. Multiply, don't add.

What are the parts of an animal cell?
Memory trick: Nine Cats Make Music.
- Nucleus: controls the cell.
- Cytoplasm: where activities happen.
- Cell membrane: controls what goes in and out.
- Mitochondria: release energy from food (respiration).

What extra parts do plant cells have?
Memory trick: Very Wobbly Cabbage.
- Vacuole: a big storage space for cell sap.
- Cell wall: made of cellulose. It is tough and gives support.
- Chloroplasts: green. They trap sunlight to make food (photosynthesis).

What is a specialised cell?
A cell with a special job. Its shape helps it do that job.
- Root hair cell: a long, thin arm gives a big surface to take in water. It has no chloroplasts, because there is no light underground.
- Muscle cell: can squeeze to help you move. It has lots of mitochondria, because moving needs lots of energy.

What does unicellular mean?
Made of just one cell. "Uni" means one. Some pond creatures are one cell: they have chloroplasts like a plant, but no cell wall, and they swim like an animal.
