# 7Fc Acidity and alkalinity — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **pH "p":** the script never says what the p stands for. Atomy says "Even scientists argue about what the p means!" The book (p92) does say the capital H stands for hydrogen, so the script uses that line.
- ⚑ **pH range:** the book page says 0 to 14, while the glossary says 1 to 14. The script teaches 0 to 14, the page version.
- ⚑ **Hook "stomach acid can attack metal":**
  - The book gives stomach acid pH 1–2 (p98) and shows corrosive diamonds at the acid end of chart B (p92). It does not mention metal; that is general KS3 knowledge (hydrochloric acid attacks some metals).
  - The script says "strong enough to attack some metals".
  - ◇ The answer (a thick lining of mucus, and the lining keeps renewing) is beyond the book and marked as a bonus.
- **Chart B positions follow the book's picture** (approximate): stomach acid 1, vinegar 2–3, fizzy drinks 3, rainwater 5, pure water 7, sea water 8, soap 9, toothpaste 10, washing powder 11, hair dye 12, oven cleaner 13–14.
  - ⚑ The book's chart draws "pure water" between 6 and 7. The script says pure water is on step 7 (neutral), which is correct.
  - ⚑ Rain is said to be "slightly acidic", not given a number.
- ◇ **Cola vs lemon juice** (pH about 2.4 vs 2.2, JADA 2016) is used once on the staircase (Fc04_13). It is beyond the book.
- **Each pH step is ten times stronger:** this is not said. It is for a grown-ups' Short only (plan fix 7).
- **Sørensen:** Danish (1868–1939), Carlsberg Laboratory, Copenhagen, 1909. He was studying brewing proteins; this extra is ◇ but accurate.
- **Universal indicator colours:** the precise colours depend on the brand (book). The script uses the book's six: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple.
- **Home lab:** cabbage juice from 7Fb, kitchen items only. Atomy says cabbage's neutral colour is purple, not green.

- **Size:** 160 spoken lines (Atomy 145, Bolt 15), 33 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1800 words. That is roughly 20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Fa.

**World:** the Rainbow Staircase is revealed in full for the first time.
- It has 15 glowing steps, 0 to 14.
- Step 7 is the calm green landing.
- Going down: warm colours and the Sour Cellar.
- Going up: cool colours and the Soapy Attic.
- Litmus could only say "upstairs" or "downstairs". Now Atomy gets step numbers.
- **Case:** "The case of the stomach that doesn't eat itself" (the 7Fb teaser).
- **Answered at the end:** stomach acid is on step 1 or 2, very acidic. ◇ A thick layer of slimy mucus protects the stomach wall, and the lining keeps renewing.

**Main tricks:**
- "Acid Down, Alkali Up, Seven in the Middle." (point to the floor, then the ceiling, then hand on heart; the unit chant)
- "Red Owls Yawn, Green Bears Purr." (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple; the owl yawns and the bear purrs)
- "Low number, Lots of acid." (crouch down low)
