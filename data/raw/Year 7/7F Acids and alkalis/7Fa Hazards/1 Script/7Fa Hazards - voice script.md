# 7Fa Hazards — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Hazard symbols:** only the current red-edged diamonds (GHS/CLP) are drawn and named. The book still shows two old orange squares (photo D, p86, and photo A, p100). The animation redraws those bottles with diamonds.
- The book teaches 6 symbols, and so does the script. The other 3 current diamonds (gas under pressure, serious health hazard, oxidising) get one "did you know" line (Fa03_16–17) and are never tested.
- ◇ **"Every Tiny Cat Eats Fish Carefully"** (Environment, Toxic, Corrosive, Explosive, Flammable, Caution) is an Atomy-made mnemonic, not from the book.
- ◇ **Cola** "nearly as acidic as lemon juice" (Fa05_17) is beyond the book (pH about 2.4 vs 2.2, JADA 2016). Cut that sentence to stay strictly in the book.
- ⚑ **Paracelsus:** "the dose makes the poison" is a later summary of his idea. The script says "often summed up like this", never quotes him.
- ⚑ **Turpentine** (book Q7): its labels can carry several diamonds. The script uses the book's three clues: pond life → environment, catches fire → flammable, headaches and sickness → caution. Some real labels also show the serious-health diamond, which is not taught here.
- **Teacher-only demo** (book p89, concentrated sulfuric acid and sugar): mentioned as a teacher demo in a fume cupboard only. No details of the reaction are given.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - the book caption "nearly 4000 children die each year in Europe" (p85). The script says "keep out of reach of young children" instead.
  - any bleach-mixing incident (grown-ups' content only).
- **Home lab:** the hunt is look-only. A grown-up holds and turns every bottle, and the child never opens, sniffs or mixes anything.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Fa Chemistry in the home (p85, unit opener), 7Fa Hazards (p86–87), 7Fa Controlling risk (p88) and Working Scientifically: Planning for hazards and risks (p89). Celly School's first lesson of unit 7F.
- **Size:** 166 spoken lines (Atomy 149, Bolt 17), 35 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1760 words. That is roughly 20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar** (same as 7E): Atomy asks a prediction, then Bolt gives a confident wrong idea, then the demo tests it on screen. Next the Zoom Lens or Captain Caution shows why, then a memory trick or gesture, then quick retrieval. Bolt is never mocked. He reboots happily and gets the true-or-false right by the end.

**World:** the Rainbow Staircase, steps 0 to 14, with step 7 as the calm green landing. Captain Caution is the safety guard, and he carries the six hazard diamonds.
- **Case:** "The case of the two-faced acid" (the 7Ee teaser: "Vinegar is safe on chips. So how can acid burn?").
- **Answered at the end:** chip-shop vinegar is only 5–8% acid. The same acid made stronger becomes an irritant, then corrosive. Concentration (the dose) makes the danger.

**Main tricks:**
- "Every Tiny Cat Eats Fish Carefully." (the six diamonds; draw a diamond in the air)
- "Corrosive Chews. Irritant Itches." (chomp hand; scratch arm)
- "The Hazard is the crocodile. The Risk is how close you get. The Precaution is the fence." (snapping jaws; jaws move closer; flat-hand fence)
- "Waft, don't Whiff." (gentle hand wave towards the nose)
- Unit chant, introduced in 7Fc: "Acid Down, Alkali Up, Seven in the Middle."
