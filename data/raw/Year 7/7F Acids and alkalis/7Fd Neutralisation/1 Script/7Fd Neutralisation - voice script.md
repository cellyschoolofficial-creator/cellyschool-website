# 7Fd Neutralisation — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- **Lime:** the book calls lime "an alkali called lime" (p94), so the script calls it an alkali.
  - The hook's "crushed white rock" is ◇: farm lime is often ground limestone, a carbonate base.
  - The script follows the book and does not explain the chemistry of limestone.
- ⚑ **Bicarbonate fizz (home lab, scene 11):**
  - Bicarbonate of soda is a carbonate-type base, so the reaction also makes carbon dioxide. That product is not in the book's acid + alkali equation.
  - The script says "The fizz is a bonus gas, carbon dioxide. Real alkalis, like sodium hydroxide, don't fizz."
- ⚑ **Pliny the Elder:** the Roman writer described farmers spreading marl (a lime-rich clay). The claim stays general, with no quote. ◇ beyond the book.
- **Hydrangeas:** cut (owner decision, 22 Oct), to stay in the book.
- **Quality control on bleach** (book STEM p96–97): bleach is only a product being tested on screen. It is never handled, mixed or used at home.
- **Lab neutralisation** (book photo D): sodium hydroxide is added to hydrochloric acid by a scientist, in animation only.
- ◇ **"All Bees Sip Water"** comes from the kit, as a mnemonic for acid + alkali → salt + water.
- **Never at home:** the home lab uses only vinegar, cabbage juice and bicarbonate of soda, in a bowl or sink.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Fd Neutralisation (p94–95) and 7Fd The chemical industry, STEM (p96–97). Celly School's fourth lesson of unit 7F.
- **Size:** 158 spoken lines (Atomy 141, Bolt 17), 31 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1700 words. That is roughly 20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Fa.

**World:** the Rainbow Staircase. "The Meeting on the Green Landing": an acid (red, spiky) climbs up and an alkali (blue, round) climbs down. On step 7 they shake hands. Then they transform into a white salt crystal and a water drop.
- **Where the map tears:** they do not just meet. They react and are used up, and new substances form.
- **Case:** "The case of the magic white rock" (the 7Fc teaser).
- **Answered at the end:** the soil was too acidic. Lime is an alkali that neutralises the acid, bringing the soil to the pH potatoes like (5.8 to 6.5).

**Main tricks:**
- "All Bees Sip Water." (acid + alkali → salt + water; buzzing fingers, then a sip)
- "Reactants on the Left, Products on the Right. The arrow means turns into." (point left, sweep an arrow, point right)
- "Chloride from hydroChloric, Sulfate from Sulfuric, Nitrate from Nitric." (clap the ending each time)
- "One drop too many!" (hold up one finger)
- Recall: "Acid Down, Alkali Up, Seven in the Middle."
