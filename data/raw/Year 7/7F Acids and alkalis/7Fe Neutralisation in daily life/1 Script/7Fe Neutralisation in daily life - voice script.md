# 7Fe Neutralisation in daily life — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Stings:**
  - The book's numbers are kept: bee venom pH 5.0–5.5 (contains methanoic acid); wasp venom pH 6.8–6.9.
  - The book's line that the pain comes from "some of the 60 other nasty substances" is kept.
  - NHS advice is added: do not use home remedies like bicarbonate of soda on stings; wash the area, use something cold, see a doctor if worried (nhs.uk, insect bites and stings).
  - The book Fact "1100 stings could be fatal" is left out for the child.
- ⚑ **Toothpaste:** the book says "Toothpastes often contain bases". Atomy says "Many toothpastes contain a base." The script never says all toothpastes are alkaline (the RSC lists that as a myth).
- ⚑ **Eggshell home lab:**
  - Eggshell is only "a bit like" tooth enamel: the shell is calcium carbonate, while enamel is mainly a calcium phosphate mineral.
  - The bubbles are a bonus gas, carbon dioxide (acid + carbonate, ◇ Year 8).
  - The toothpaste-coated extension varies by brand, so trial it before filming.
- ⚑ **Justin Schmidt** (American insect scientist, 1947–2023, Schmidt sting pain index):
  - It is told gently, with no pain detail and no sting counts.
  - The script says his work showed that venoms are mixtures of many substances.
- ⚑ **Power stations:** the book's photo E caption says coal power stations "can release sulfuric acid as a waste gas". The script says "acidic waste gases" (the book's main text), which is accurate.
- **Joseph's accident** is the book's own story (p100), told calmly: the glass got very hot, he dropped it, and an adult cleared it up safely. It is the "Never Mix, Never Risk" safety thread.
  - Bleach-mixing incidents are not mentioned (grown-ups' Short only).
- **Unit close:** a recap game across 7Fa–7Fe, then a teaser for 7G Club Particle ("You can pour sand like water. So is sand a liquid?").
- **"Have your say"** (council ban on pH above 10, p100) is left for the workbook.

- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Fa.

**World:** the Rainbow Staircase, final case of unit 7F: "Myth-busting on the Staircase". Atomy tests everyday neutralisation claims.
- Two are true chemistry: antacids and toothpaste.
- One is a myth: baking soda on stings.
- One is a danger warning: mixing cleaners.
- **Case:** "The case of the baking-soda bee sting" (the 7Fd teaser: "A bee stings! People put baking soda on it. Does that work?").
- **Answered at the end:** bee venom is a little acidic. But the pain comes from about 60 other substances in the venom, not its pH, so neutralising does not stop it. The NHS says not to use home remedies like bicarbonate on stings.

**Main tricks:**
- "Every Alkali is a Base, but not every Base is an Alkali." (big hug-circle with the arms, then a small circle with the fingers inside it)
- "Antacid means Anti-acid." (a shield arm)
- "Never Mix, Never Risk." (crossed arms in an X)
- Recall: "All Bees Sip Water." "Acid Down, Alkali Up, Seven in the Middle."
