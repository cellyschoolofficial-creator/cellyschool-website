## Script

### Scene 0
- **Ce00_01** [curious, intriguing] Here is a mystery. A young gymnast won an Olympic gold medal.
- **Ce00_02** [curious] Then she lost it. She had only taken two tablets for a cold. How?
- **Ce00_03** [warm] Today, we solve the case of the lost gold medal.

### Scene 1
- **Ce01_01** [warm, smiling] Hello, scientist! Welcome back, detective.
- **Ce01_02** [calm, recalling] Last time, we found out how muscles move your bones.
- **Ce01_03** [clear] Can a muscle push a bone?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce01_04** [pleased] No! Muscles can only pull. So they work in pairs.
- **Ce01_05** [clear] Which muscle contracts to bend your arm?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce01_06** [pleased] The biceps. The triceps contracts to straighten it again.
- **Ce01_07** [curious] Athletes train hard to make their muscles strong. But some try to cheat. How?
- **Ce01_08** [gentle] Bend your arm and feel your biceps. Now straighten it and feel your triceps.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 2
- **Ce02_01** [clear] A drug is a substance that affects the way your body works.
- **Ce02_02** [explaining] Caffeine, nicotine and alcohol are all drugs.
- **Ce02_03** [clear] Some drugs are medicines. Medicines help people recover from illness or injury.
- **Ce02_04** [explaining] Paracetamol reduces pain.
- **Ce02_05** [slow, rhythmic, one beat at a time] Pa. Ra. Ce. Ta. Mol. Five taps.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce02_06** [explaining] Ibuprofen reduces pain and swelling.
- **Ce02_07** [explaining] Decongestants are in cold medicines. They help you breathe more easily.
- **Ce02_08** [curious] A footballer strains a muscle. It hurts and swells up. Which medicine could help?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce02_09** [pleased] Ibuprofen! It reduces the pain and the swelling.
- **Ce02_10** [gentle] Hold up one finger for paracetamol. Two fingers for ibuprofen.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 3
- **Ce03_01** [explaining] Tubes called airways carry air to your lungs. Muscles wrap around them.
- **Ce03_02** [explaining] In an asthma attack, these muscles contract hard. The airways get narrow.
- **Ce03_03** [explaining] Extra mucus is made too. So it is hard to breathe.
- **Ce03_04** [clear] A medicine called salbutamol makes the muscles relax.
- **Ce03_05** [slow, rhythmic, one beat at a time] Sal. Bu. Ta. Mol. Four taps.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce03_06** [explaining] The airways get wider again. More air can get into the lungs.
- **Ce03_07** [curious] In an asthma attack, why is it hard to breathe?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce03_08** [pleased] The muscles squeeze the airways narrow. And there is extra mucus.
- **Ce03_09** [gentle] Breathe in through a tiny gap in your lips. Now open your mouth wide.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 4
- **Ce04_01** [clear] Many drugs are useful. But they can also have side-effects.
- **Ce04_02** [explaining] A side-effect is a harmful or unpleasant effect of a drug.
- **Ce04_03** [explaining] All medicines can have side-effects. Even everyday ones.
- **Ce04_04** [clear] Paracetamol can damage the liver.
- **Ce04_05** [explaining] Why the liver? Because the liver breaks drugs down.
- **Ce04_06** [clear] Too much paracetamol can badly damage the liver. Always follow the instructions.
- **Ce04_07** [curious] What is the useful effect of paracetamol? And what is its side-effect?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce04_08** [pleased] It reduces pain. But it can damage the liver.
- **Ce04_09** [explaining] New medicines are tested for a long time, to look for side-effects.
- **Ce04_10** [explaining] Long ago, heroin was put in cough medicine. Now we know it is dangerous.
- **Ce04_11** [gentle] Put your hand on the right side of your tummy, under your ribs. Your liver is there.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 5
- **Ce05_01** [clear] Some drugs are addictive.
- **Ce05_02** [slow, rhythmic, one beat at a time] Ad. Dic. Tive. Three taps.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce05_03** [explaining] Addictive means people feel they cannot manage without the drug.
- **Ce05_04** [explaining] Nicotine, in cigarettes, is addictive.
- **Ce05_05** [explaining] Smoking can cause diseases that stop blood flowing to parts of the body.
- **Ce05_06** [explaining] People who are addicted often keep using a drug. Even when they see the damage.
- **Ce05_07** [clear] Harmful use of any drug is called substance misuse, or substance abuse.
- **Ce05_08** [explaining] Substance misuse often causes brain damage and liver damage.
- **Ce05_09** [curious] Why do some people keep using a drug, even when it harms them?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce05_10** [pleased] Because they are addicted. They feel they cannot stop.
- **Ce05_11** [gentle] Take a slow, calm breath in. And out. Healthy lungs feel good.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 6
- **Ce06_01** [playful] Thumbs up if it is true. Thumbs down if it is false.
- **Ce06_02** [clear] Number one. Caffeine is not a drug.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce06_03** [pleased] Thumbs down! Caffeine affects the way your body works. So it is a drug.
- **Ce06_04** [clear] Number two. All medicines can have side-effects.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce06_05** [pleased] Thumbs up! Even paracetamol can damage the liver.
- **Ce06_06** [clear] Number three. Drugs are mainly broken down in the kidneys.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce06_07** [pleased] Thumbs down! Drugs are mainly broken down in the liver.
- **Ce06_08** [warm, proud] Brilliant detective work. Part one is done. Time for a break!

### Scene 7
- **Ce07_01** [warm] Welcome back, detective. Now, how do drugs change the body?
- **Ce07_02** [explaining] Some drugs are taken for pleasure, not as medicines. These are recreational drugs.
- **Ce07_03** [clear] Caffeine is a legal recreational drug. Cola has caffeine in it.
- **Ce07_04** [explaining] Other recreational drugs are illegal, because their side-effects are so harmful.
- **Ce07_05** [explaining] Cannabis can cause memory loss and mental illness.
- **Ce07_06** [explaining] Ecstasy can cause mental illness, kidney problems, and even death.
- **Ce07_07** [explaining] Cocaine can cause blocked arteries. It can also damage the nose.
- **Ce07_08** [explaining] Heroin can cause collapsed veins, vomiting and severe headaches.
- **Ce07_09** [curious] A glass of cola sits next to some medicines. Which recreational drug is in the cola?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce07_10** [pleased] Caffeine!

### Scene 8
- **Ce08_01** [explaining] Many drugs affect your nervous system.
- **Ce08_02** [explaining] Your nervous system controls your body with electrical signals called impulses.
- **Ce08_03** [clear] Some drugs make impulses travel faster. These drugs are called stimulants.
- **Ce08_04** [slow, rhythmic, one beat at a time] Stim. U. Lants. Three taps.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce08_05** [explaining] Stimulants can decrease your reaction time.
- **Ce08_06** [explaining] Reaction time is how long you take to respond to something.
- **Ce08_07** [explaining] Caffeine, cocaine and ecstasy are all stimulants.
- **Ce08_08** [clear] Here is a trick. Stimulants Speed things up.
- **Ce08_09** [curious] Why do some people feel more awake after drinking coffee?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce08_10** [pleased] Coffee has caffeine. Caffeine is a stimulant. It speeds up the impulses.
- **Ce08_11** [gentle] Clap your hands as fast as you can when I say go. Ready? Go!
*(long pause)*

### Scene 9
- **Ce09_01** [clear] Other drugs make impulses travel more slowly. These are called depressants.
- **Ce09_02** [slow, rhythmic, one beat at a time] De. Press. Ants. Three taps.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce09_03** [explaining] Depressants make your reaction time longer. That is dangerous when driving.
- **Ce09_04** [explaining] Heroin is a dangerous depressant. So are solvents in glues and paints.
- **Ce09_05** [explaining] Solvents can stop the heart and lungs working. They can cause severe brain damage.
- **Ce09_06** [clear] Alcohol is a depressant too.
- **Ce09_07** [explaining] It stops parts of the brain working. People may become loud and aggressive.
- **Ce09_08** [explaining] Alcohol can also cause vomiting and liver damage.
- **Ce09_09** [curious] Where are your breathing muscles?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce09_10** [pleased] Your diaphragm, under your lungs. And the muscles between your ribs.
- **Ce09_11** [explaining] Too much alcohol can stop impulses reaching the breathing muscles. Then breathing stops.
- **Ce09_12** [encouraging] Say the trick with me. Stimulants Speed. Depressants Drag.
- **Ce09_13** [gentle] Move your hand fast, then very slowly. Speed, then drag.
*(long pause)*

### Scene 10
- **Ce10_01** [explaining] Now, back to sport. Athletes are tested for drugs, to stop cheating.
- **Ce10_02** [explaining] Some athletes take steroids, to make their muscles grow bigger.
- **Ce10_03** [explaining] Testosterone is a steroid. It is a sex hormone, made in the testes and ovaries.
- **Ce10_04** [explaining] Steroids are drugs, because they affect the way the body works.
- **Ce10_05** [explaining] A side-effect is that people can become more aggressive.
- **Ce10_06** [explaining] Muscles grow fast and pull harder on the bones. So bones can get stress fractures.
- **Ce10_07** [clear] Other athletes cheat with a chemical called E P O.
- **Ce10_08** [explaining] E P O makes the bone marrow make more red blood cells.
- **Ce10_09** [explaining] Red blood cells carry oxygen. More oxygen reaches the muscles for respiration.
- **Ce10_10** [explaining] So the muscles get more energy. They can work harder for longer.
- **Ce10_11** [explaining] A famous cyclist used E P O and testosterone. His medals were taken away.
- **Ce10_12** [gentle] Make a strong muscle with both arms. Real strength comes from training!
*(long pause)*

### Scene 11
- **Ce11_01** [clear] Sometimes it is hard to decide what is cheating.
- **Ce11_02** [explaining] Salbutamol is banned in sport. It widens the airways, so more air gets in.
- **Ce11_03** [explaining] A rugby player was banned for using it. He proved he needed it for asthma, so his ban was lifted.
- **Ce11_04** [curious] Now, our mystery. Why did two cold tablets cost a gymnast her gold medal?
- **Ce11_05** [explaining] The tablets had a decongestant in them. Decongestants are drugs.
- **Ce11_06** [explaining] Some decongestants are stimulants. They can speed up impulses and help breathing.
- **Ce11_07** [warm] So the drugs test found it. She lost her medal, even though she had a cold.
- **Ce11_08** [curious] Should all drugs be banned in sport? Science cannot answer that question.
- **Ce11_09** [explaining] It depends on what people think is right or wrong. It is an ethical question.
- **Ce11_10** [playful] Detective game! Stimulant or depressant?
- **Ce11_11** [clear] Number one. Caffeine.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce11_12** [pleased] Stimulant! It speeds up impulses.
- **Ce11_13** [clear] Number two. Alcohol.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce11_14** [pleased] Depressant! It slows down impulses.
- **Ce11_15** [clear] Number three. Is ibuprofen a medicine, or a recreational drug?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce11_16** [pleased] A medicine! It reduces pain and swelling.

### Scene 12
- **Ce12_01** [upbeat, friendly] Quiz time! Say the answer out loud.
- **Ce12_02** [clear] Question one. What is a drug?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce12_03** [pleased] A substance that affects the way your body works.
- **Ce12_04** [clear] Question two. Which organ breaks drugs down?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce12_05** [pleased] The liver.
- **Ce12_06** [clear] Question three. What does a stimulant do to impulses?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce12_07** [pleased] It makes them travel faster. Stimulants Speed.
- **Ce12_08** [clear] Question four. Name a depressant.
*(long pause)*
- **Ce12_09** [pleased] Alcohol, heroin, or solvents. Depressants Drag.
- **Ce12_10** [clear] Question five. Why do some athletes take steroids?
*(long pause)*
- **Ce12_11** [pleased] To make their muscles grow bigger.
- **Ce12_12** [warm, calm] Here are three big ideas.
- **Ce12_13** [warm, calm] One. A drug changes the way your body works. Medicines are drugs too.
- **Ce12_14** [warm, calm] Two. All drugs can have side-effects. Some are addictive.
- **Ce12_15** [warm, calm] Three. Stimulants speed up impulses. Depressants slow them down.
- **Ce12_16** [warm] Case solved, detective! A cold medicine had a drug that is banned in sport.
- **Ce12_17** [warm] Next time, we start a new case. We explore ecosystems, and how living things depend on each other.
- **Ce12_18** [warm, cheerful] Great work today. See you next time, scientist!
