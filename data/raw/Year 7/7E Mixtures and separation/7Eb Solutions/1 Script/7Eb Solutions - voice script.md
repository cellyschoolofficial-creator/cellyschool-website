# 7Eb Solutions — voice script

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Eb Solutions (the two pages after 7Ea Mixtures), with Workbook 7Eb and Activity Pack 7Eb-1 to 7Eb-3. Celly School's second chemistry lesson. 165 spoken lines (Atomy 146, Bolt 19), 37 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1837 words, roughly 20–21 minutes with pauses. Host: **Atomy the atom** (his own voice, not Celly's). Sidekick: **Bolt** the robot (his own small, bright robot voice). Sentences are 15 words or fewer.
Lesson grammar (same as 7Ea): Atomy asks a prediction, Bolt gives a confident wrong idea, the demo tests it on screen, the Zoom Lens shows why at particle level, then a memory trick or gesture, then quick retrieval. Bolt is never mocked; he reboots happily and gets the true-or-false right by the end.
World: Atomy's Unmixing Lab. Case: "The case of the vanishing sugar" (the 7Ea teaser: "Next time, sugar vanishes in a cup of tea. Is it really gone?"). Evidence tool: kitchen scales. Answered at the end: the scales read 170 g, so the sugar is still there.
Main tricks: "The solute is the cute one that hides in the crowd; the solvent is the crowd" (pinch, then arms wide). "Mass Never Melts away" (level-hands scales). "Saturated means Stuffed" (pat tummy). "Warm holds more" (rub hands). Word-part: sol means dissolve.
