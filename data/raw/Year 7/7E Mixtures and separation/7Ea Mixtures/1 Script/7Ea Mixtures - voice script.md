# 7Ea Mixtures — voice script

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book p69 (unit opener), p70–71 (STEM: Forensic science) and p72–73 (Mixtures). Celly School's first chemistry lesson and the showcase for the new creative approach. 155 spoken lines (Celly 137, Bolt 18), 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1700 words, roughly 19 minutes with pauses. Voice: Sulafat (Gemini Flash TTS) for Celly; Bolt needs his own small, bright robot voice. No sentence is longer than 15 words.
New lesson grammar in every teaching scene: Celly asks a prediction, then Bolt gives a confident wrong idea, then the demo tests it on screen, then the Zoom Lens shows why at particle level, then a memory trick, then quick retrieval.
Bolt is a small, confident, funny robot (the same Bolt from the 7Aa clinic game). He is never mocked: he is delighted to be proved wrong ("My circuits are rebooting!"), and by the end he gets the true-or-false and quiz ideas right.
World: Celly's Unmixing Lab, a forensic crime lab where every mixture is a crime scene and every separation tool catches suspects by one difference. Hook: the muddy river that becomes clear tap water, answered at the end.
Main tricks: "Solutions are See-through. Suspensions Sink. Colloids Cloud." and "Residue Rests. Filtrate Flows.", both with gestures. Unit teaser only: "Six Fish Eat Delicious Crumbs".
