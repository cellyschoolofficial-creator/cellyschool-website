# 7Hc Metals and non-metals — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Aluminium, the metal of kings** (hook and true story, Hc00, Hc10): the book says that in the late 19th century aluminium was called the metal of kings, as it was more expensive to produce than gold, and that it now makes 200 billion drinks cans a year. The history goes beyond the book ◇: in 1886 Charles Martin Hall (USA) and Paul Héroult (France) each found a cheap way to extract it using electricity. The story that Napoleon III gave his most honoured guests aluminium cutlery is widely told but hard to source, so the script says "the story goes".
- ◇ **"Metals Shine During Cold Hours"** (malleable, shiny, ductile, conduct, high melting point) is from the kit. **"Mallet-able"** is an Atomy-made word-part trick (*malleus* is Latin for hammer).
- ⚑ **Graphite** conducts electricity (book: used in batteries and electric motors). The script does not show a pencil line lighting a bulb.
- ⚑ **Mercury** is toxic. The script shows it only as the liquid in old thermometers and says "never touch mercury".
- **Copper mine** (book table E): costs per 1000 kg of ore are survey £11, mining £19.50, transport £10, extracting £31, admin and sales £14 and distribution £4.50, which makes £90. The ore is 1% copper, so 10 kg of copper; at £4.50 a kg that sells for £45, a loss. The selling price must be £9 a kg to break even (Activity Pack answers).
- **Ores** (book photo D): magnetite is 72% iron, limonite 55%. 200 g of magnetite gives 144 g of iron (answers).
- ⚑ **Magnet home lab:** many "copper" coins are copper-plated steel and are magnetic, and some food tins are steel. The script calls these "surprises", not rules. Use only a fridge magnet (no strong magnets), and keep it away from phones and bank cards.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Hc Metals and non-metals (p124–125) and 7Hc Obtaining metals (STEM, p126). Celly School's third lesson of 7H.
- **Size:** 155 spoken lines (Atomy 141, Bolt 14), 32 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1630 words. That is roughly 19–20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** Element City has two districts. The shiny Metal District fills about three quarters of the map. The Non-metal Quarter is smaller. Three rule-breakers don't fit their district: mercury, silicon and graphite.
- **Case:** "The case of the metal of kings" (the 7Hb teaser: "the metal of kings. You probably throw some away every week!").
- **Answered at the end:** aluminium. It was very hard to get out of its compounds, so it cost more than gold. Once a cheap way to extract it was found, it became an everyday metal, used for 200 billion drinks cans a year.

**Main tricks:**
- "Metals Shine During Cold Hours." Malleable, Shiny, Ductile, Conduct, High melting point. (hammer fist / sparkle fingers / pull a string / zap / thermometer up)
- "Only Iron, Nickel and Cobalt cling." (a fist that clings to the other palm)
- "Malleable is Mallet-able." (you can hit it with a mallet)
