# 7He Chemical reactions — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Joseph Priestley, 1 August 1774** (true story, He10) goes beyond the book ◇. He focused sunlight with a large burning lens onto mercury oxide and collected the gas, and a candle burned very brightly in it. Lavoisier later named it oxygen. The Swedish chemist Carl Scheele made oxygen a little earlier but published later, so the script says "one of the first people to make oxygen".
- ⚑ **Mercury is toxic** (book: Problems with elements). The script says "today, scientists never heat mercury compounds in the open".
- ⚑ **Balloon gas catcher home lab** (He11): vinegar and bicarbonate of soda take heat in, so the bottle gets slightly **colder**, not hotter. The script calls this "a temperature change, a bonus clue". Use a tray, sunglasses or goggles, don't point it at faces, no tasting.
- ⚑ **"Bubbles always mean a reaction"** (Bolt, He02): ◇ boiling water bubbles too, but no new substance forms. This is a fair KS3 nuance.
- ⚑ **Mining in low-income countries** (He09): the book raises benefits, problems and child labour. The script mentions child labour once, gently and without detail, because the viewers are children.
- ◇ **"Cats Get Sleepy Here"** (Colour change, Gas, Solid forms, Heat change), **"Heat Hits, Compound Cracks"**, **"Limewater goes Milky"** and **"-IDE, I'm made of two. -ATE, I ATE some oxygen"** are from the kit.
- **Unit finale** (He12): "The glass of water, case closed" recaps 7E, 7F, 7G and 7H. Atomy hosts it.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7He Chemical reactions (p129–130) and 7He Problems with elements (p131). Celly School's last lesson of 7H, with the unit finale.
- **Size:** 156 spoken lines (Atomy 143, Bolt 13), 33 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1580 words. That is roughly 19–20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga, plus a unit finale at the end of Scene 12.

**World:** Element City's Swap Dance. In a reaction, citizens let go of old partners and take new ones. No citizen is added, and none is lost. Sometimes a citizen floats away as a gas.
- **Case:** "The case of the vanishing gas" (the 7Hd teaser: "a green powder turns black when heated. Where does the gas go?").
- **Answered at the end:** green copper carbonate breaks down when heated. It makes black copper oxide and carbon dioxide gas. The gas escapes into the air, and limewater catches it and goes milky.

**Main tricks:**
- "Cats Get Sleepy Here." Colour change, Gas, Solid forms, Heat change. (paint a rainbow / bubbles with fingers / clap / fan the face)
- "Heat Hits, Compound Cracks." (a flame hand, then break a stick)
- "-IDE, I'm made of two. -ATE, I ATE some oxygen." (two fingers / pat the tummy)
- "Limewater goes Milky." (pour a glass, then wiggle fingers like clouds)
