# 7Je Using electricity — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Fuse choice** (Je07, quiz): the book asks which of four standard fuses (photo D) suits an appliance with a current of 6 A. The design doc gives the answer key's fuses as 3 A, 5 A, 10 A and 13 A, and the answer 10 A ("just above the normal current"). Check this against the Activity Pack answer before recording. Everyday UK plugs most often use 3 A, 5 A and 13 A fuses.
- ⚑ **True story** (Je10): the book says "the world's first coal-fired public power station was opened in London in 1882". The script adds ◇ that it was at Holborn Viaduct and supplied nearby street lamps and buildings, which is well documented. It avoids saying it lit "all of London". The book's p164 also says the first power stations were built near the end of the 1800s.
- ⚑ **Current through the body** (Je03): the book says you can feel 1 mA as a tingle and that 1 A through your body is likely to kill. The script keeps this calm and factual for children: "a tiny current tingles; a big current can stop the heart".
- ⚑ **What a fuse protects** (◇): the book says a fuse melts and breaks the circuit if the current is too high. The script adds once that this helps stop wires overheating and causing fires (book: wires that get too hot may cause fires).
- ⚑ **Cable colours outside the UK:** the book says the insulation colours are used in many countries. The home lab is look-only and never asks the child to open a plug.
- **Safety (home lab):** look, don't touch. Never open a plug, never touch sockets, never handle anything plugged in. Mains electricity can kill.
- **Unit finale** (scene 12): a short recap of 7Ja–7Je, then the 7K Forces teaser (the astronaut on the Moon, from the physics design doc).
- ◇ **"Brown is busy. Blue is back. Green and yellow is ground."**, **"Fuse: just above, not way above"** and **"Water and wires? Walk away."** are Volty-made tricks.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Je Using electricity (p162–163) and 7Je A world without electricity (p164). Celly School's fifth and last lesson of 7J Current electricity.
- **Size:** 132 spoken lines (Volty 121, Bolt 11), 24 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1330 words. That is roughly 16–17 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Volty the spark (voice Autonoe).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ia.

**World:** The Mains Gate. Behind a locked safety door is a huge, high-voltage version of the Bead Loop: the mains. Its Pusher is enormous. Volty never goes in. He watches through thick glass, with his safety shield up. Inside a plug, a tiny fuse hero in a cape guards the loop.
- **Case:** "The case of the wire that breaks on purpose" (the 7Jd teaser).
- **Answered at the end:** it is a fuse. If the current gets too big, the thin fuse wire melts and breaks the circuit. That stops the current before wires overheat and cause a fire.

**Main tricks:**
- "Brown is busy. Blue is back. Green and yellow is ground." (live, neutral, earth) A zig-zag hand / a flat calm hand / point to the ground.
- "Fuse: just above, not way above." One hand hovers just above the other.
- "Water and wires? Walk away." Three steps back.
