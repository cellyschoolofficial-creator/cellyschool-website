# 7Gd Diffusion — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Durian ban:** the book shows a sign banning durians (and pets), with a fine, and says durian is banned from most public places because of its smell. The script says "on some trains" and names no country.
- ◇ **The smelly gas in cooking gas** (true story, Gd09): natural gas has no smell, so suppliers add a tiny amount of a very smelly chemical that smells of rotten eggs or cabbage. If gas leaks, it diffuses and warns people. This is accurate but not in the book. The 1937 New London school explosion that led to the law is left out.
- ⚑ **Thomas Graham** (Scotland, 1830s–1840s) measured how fast different gases diffuse. Two lines only (Gd09_08, Gd09_10: "some gases diffuse faster than others"; the Activity Pack 7Gd-7 balloon sheet also shows helium escaping faster than carbon dioxide). Cut if you prefer to stay in the book.
- ◇ **Warm water diffuses faster** (Gd08, home lab): the Activity Pack (7Gd-9) says particles move faster at higher temperatures and so mix faster. Not in the Student Book itself.
- ◇ **"Particles keep moving when the mixing is even"** (Gd06_08–09): a standard KS3 fix. The book says the gases mix "until the gases in both gas jars look the same".
- ◇ **Tricks:** "Crowded to Clear", "High to low, off they go, no push, no blow" and "Gases Gallop, Liquids Linger, Solids Snail" are Atomy-made.
- **Home lab:** warm tap water poured by a grown-up, never boiling. Glasses on a towel; food colouring stains. The smell race uses an orange.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Gd Diffusion (p112–113). Celly School's fourth lesson of unit 7G.
- **Size:** 160 spoken lines (Atomy 143, Bolt 17), 35 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1680 words. That is roughly 20 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** Club Particle. "The case of the sneaky stink": smelly landfill-gas dancers sneak onto the club's floors. They spread fastest on Floor G (gas), slower on Floor L (liquid), and slowest of all through Floor S (solid).
- **Case:** "The case of the sneaky stink" (the 7Gc teaser: "A smelly fruit is banned on trains. How does a smell cross a carriage?").
- **Answered at the end:** diffusion. The smell particles are always moving, in all directions. They spread out between the air particles, from where there are lots (near the fruit) to where there are few, until they reach your nose. No wind or push is needed.

**Main tricks:**
- "Crowded to Clear." Diffusion goes from high concentration to low. (bunch your hands together, then spread them wide)
- "High to low, off they go. No push, no blow."
- "Gases Gallop, Liquids Linger, Solids Snail." (galloping hands; a slow wave; a finger crawls like a snail)
- Recall from 7Gc: "Random hits, Random path." "Bumped, not Brave."
