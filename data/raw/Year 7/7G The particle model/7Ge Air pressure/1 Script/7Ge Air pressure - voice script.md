# 7Ge Air pressure — voice script

**Check before recording (flags)**
- ⚑ **Magdeburg hemispheres** (hook and true story, Ge00, Ge09): Otto von Guericke's 1654 demonstration is accurate but not in the book. The number of horses differs between accounts (a later version has two teams of eight). The script says "teams of horses" and never gives a number. The hemispheres are called "two metal half-bowls".
- ◇ **Shrinking bottle** (home lab, Ge11_07–10): a grown-up swirls hot tap water (never boiling) in a plastic bottle, pours it out, closes the lid and lets it cool. It crumples because the air inside cools and its particles hit the walls less. This goes beyond the book; the script explains it simply as "fewer, slower hits inside".
- ◇ **Upside-down card trick** (home lab): standard air-pressure demo, done over a sink. Not in the book.
- ⚑ **Weather:** the book says "high air pressure usually produces more settled weather". The script keeps "usually".
- ◇ **"Straws don't Suck, the air Shoves"**, **"More Hits, More Push"** and **"Vacuum means Very empty"** are Atomy-made tricks.
- **Fizzy drink Fact** (book): "a 1 litre bottle of most fizzy drinks contains nearly 3 litres of pressurised carbon dioxide gas. This gas pressure can sometimes cause these bottles to explode." The script keeps the 3 litres and says bottles "can sometimes burst" (Ge05_03).
- **Landfill methane** (book): it is flammable, builds up pressure, can crack the surface and can explode with sparks. The script keeps it calm: "piped out and burned safely".
- **Unit close:** a recap game across 7Ga–7Ge, then a teaser for 7H Element City (Rayleigh's "heavy nitrogen" and the hidden gas, argon).
- **Home lab:** the card trick is done over a sink. The hot-bottle step is for a grown-up only, with hot tap water, never boiling water in plastic.

Pearson Exploring Science International, Year 7, Student Book 7Ge Air pressure (p114–115), 7Ge Forecasting the weather (STEM, p116) and 7Ge Waste (p117, unit closer). Celly School's fifth lesson and the 7G unit finale.
- **Size:** 160 spoken lines (Atomy 143, Bolt 17), 34 long pauses, 13 scenes (hook + 12), 2 parts, about 1750 words. That is roughly 20–21 minutes with pauses.
- **Host:** Atomy the atom (voice Zephyr).
- **Sidekick:** Bolt the robot (voice Puck).
- **Sentence length:** 15 words or fewer.

**Lesson grammar:** same as 7Ga.

**World:** Club Particle, final night. On Floor G, the Bumper Zoom, the dancers keep crashing into the walls. Atomy realises every crash is a tiny push. More dancers means more pushes. A floor with no dancers at all is a vacuum.
- **Case:** "The case of the invisible push" (the 7Gd teaser: "Teams of horses can't pull apart two empty metal bowls. What holds them?").
- **Answered at the end:** air pressure. The air was pumped out from inside the bowls, leaving almost a vacuum. Billions of air particles kept hitting the outside and pushing the halves together, with nothing inside pushing back.

**Main tricks:**
- "More Hits, More Push." (drum your fingers on the desk faster and faster)
- "Vacuum means Very empty. No dancers at all." (show two empty palms)
- "Straws don't Suck. The air Shoves." (pretend to sip, then push a palm upwards)
- Unit recap: "Solids Shake. Liquids Slide. Gases Go everywhere." "Bumped, not Brave." "Crowded to Clear."
