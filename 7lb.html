Memory tricks

No particles, no party.
The energy travels. The particles stay.
Steel speeds. Water wades. Air ambles.

Three big ideas

  1. Sound needs a medium. It cannot travel through a vacuum.
  2. Sound is a pressure wave. It transfers energy, not particles.
  3. Sound is fastest in solids, slower in liquids, slowest in gases.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What does sound need to travel through?

A medium! A solid, a liquid or a gas.

Where does sound travel fastest: steel, water or air?

Steel! Steel speeds. Water wades. Air ambles.

Do air particles travel from the drum to your ear?

No! Only the nudge travels. The energy travels. The particles stay.

One wave takes nought point two seconds. What is the frequency?

Five hertz! Five waves pass each second.
