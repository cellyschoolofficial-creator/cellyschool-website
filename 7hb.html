Memory tricks

Big letter Boss, little letter follows. Two capitals, two elements.
Oxygen and Silicon Own the crust.
Same atoms, different joins.

Three big ideas

  1. Symbols have one or two letters. The first is always a capital.
  2. Most elements in nature are found joined in compounds.
  3. Properties depend on the atoms and how they are arranged.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Why do scientists use symbols for elements?

Every scientist in the world understands them, whatever language they speak.

Which two elements make up most of the Earth's crust?

Oxygen and silicon! O and Si.

Give two ways to say what an element is.

It has one kind of atom. And it can't be broken down into anything simpler.

Diamond and graphite are both carbon. Why are they different?

Their atoms are joined in different ways. Same atoms, different joins.

Why must we recycle old phones?

Some elements are running out. Recycling saves them.
