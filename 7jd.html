Memory tricks

Ammeter along. Voltmeter across.

Three big ideas

  1. Voltage is the push. A higher voltage gives a bigger current.
  2. Resistance makes it harder for current to flow.
  3. In parallel, more branches mean less resistance and a bigger current.

How is a voltmeter connected?
