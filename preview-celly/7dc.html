Three big ideas

  1. Environmental factors cause environmental variation. Like light, wind and fashion.
  2. Living things are adapted to daily changes. Nocturnal animals are active at night.
  3. They are adapted to seasonal changes. Deciduous Drops. Hibernate hides. Migrate moves.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What causes environmental variation?

Environmental factors. Like light, wind, fertiliser, or fashion.

What does nocturnal mean?

Active only at night. Like owls, and many mice.

Why do deciduous trees lose their leaves in winter?

So they do not lose water when the ground is frozen.

What is hibernation?

Being inactive in winter, so an animal does not need food.

Why do a ptarmigan's feathers change colour?

To stay camouflaged in each season.
