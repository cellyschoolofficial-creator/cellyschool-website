Three big ideas

  1. A habitat's environment comes from physical factors, like light, wetness, wind and temperature.
  2. Adaptations help organisms survive. Small ears save heat. Spines save water.
  3. Features are inherited. Each gamete has a different mix, so living things vary.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Name three physical environmental factors.

Light, temperature and wind. Wetness counts too.

What is an adaptation?

A feature that helps an organism survive in its habitat.

What is an ecosystem?

A community, plus all the physical factors in a habitat.

Give one polar bear adaptation.

White fur, thick fur, small ears or big feet.

Why does each child inherit a different mix of features?

Each gamete has a slightly different mix of instructions.
