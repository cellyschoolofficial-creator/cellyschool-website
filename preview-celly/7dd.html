Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

resources

ReSourCes

population

PopULaTion

extinct

ExTinct

competition

ComPeTiTion

There are not enough resources for everyone. So organisms compete for them. This is competition.

producer

ProDuCer

Grass makes its own food, using light. So it is a producer.

consumer

ConSuMer

Animals cannot make their own food. They eat other organisms. They are consumers.

predator

PredATor

An animal that hunts other animals is a predator. The animal it hunts is its prey.

biotic

BiOtIc

interdependent

InTerDePenDent

We say they are interdependent.

Memory tricks

Silly Frogs Sing With My Owl. Shelter, food, space, water, mates, oxygen.
Inter is like international, between countries. Intra means inside one species.
Producers produce food. Consumers consume it. Consume means eat.
No plants, no web. The whole food web depends on the producers.

Three big ideas

  1. Living things need resources. If one is missing, the population goes down.
  2. Organisms compete for resources. Food webs show what eats what.
  3. Living things are interdependent. Change one population, and others change too.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Name three resources that animals need.

Any three. Shelter, food, space, water, mates or oxygen.

What does extinct mean?

There are none of that species left. Like the Easter Island palm.

What is inter-specific competition?

Competition between different species.

In grass, hare, lynx, which animal is the predator?

The lynx! The hare is its prey.

What does interdependent mean?

Organisms depend on each other. Not just for food.
