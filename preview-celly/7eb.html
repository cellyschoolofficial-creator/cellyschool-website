Three big ideas

  1. A solute dissolves in a solvent to make a solution. It is still there, just too small to see.
  2. Dissolving does not change the mass. Mass Never Melts away.
  3. A saturated solution is Stuffed. Warmer solvents usually hold more solute.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

In sweet tea, which is the solute, and which is the solvent?

Sugar is the solute. Tea is the solvent. The cute one hides in the crowd.

What does insoluble mean?

It cannot dissolve. Like sand in water.

Twenty grams of sugar goes into one hundred and fifty grams of tea. What is the total mass?

One hundred and seventy grams! Mass Never Melts away.

Salt sits at the bottom and will not dissolve. What kind of solution is that?

Saturated! Saturated means Stuffed.

Does more copper sulfate dissolve in warm water, or in cold water?

Warm water! Warm holds more.
