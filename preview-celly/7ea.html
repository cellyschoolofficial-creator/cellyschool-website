Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

mixture

MixTure

Muddy water is a mixture.

solution

SoLuTion

suspension

SusPenSion

A mixture that separates if you stop stirring is called a suspension.

colloid

ColLoid

Milk is a colloid. One substance is spread out in another. They don't separate easily.

filtration

FilTraTion

residue

ResIDue

The sand left in the paper is called the residue.

filtrate

FilTrate

The liquid that passes through is called the filtrate.

Memory tricks

Solutions are See-through. Suspensions Sink. Colloids Cloud.
Cup your hand. Residue Rests. Trickle your fingers down. Filtrate Flows.

Three big ideas

  1. A mixture is substances jumbled together, but not joined. We can separate them.
  2. Solutions are See-through. Suspensions Sink. Colloids Cloud.
  3. Filtering separates insoluble solids. Residue Rests. Filtrate Flows.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a mixture?

Two or more substances jumbled together, but not joined.

Is sea water a solution, a suspension or a colloid?

A solution! Solutions are See-through.

Sand sinks in water if you stop stirring. What kind of mixture is that?

A suspension! Suspensions Sink.

In filtering, what is the liquid that passes through called?

The filtrate! Filtrate Flows.

Why is chlorine added to drinking water?

To kill microorganisms. They are too small to filter out.
