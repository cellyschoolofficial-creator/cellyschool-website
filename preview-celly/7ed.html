Three big ideas

  1. Chromatography separates dissolved substances. The solvent carries them at different speeds.
  2. The pattern is a chromatogram. Count the spots, count the dyes.
  3. Matching patterns helps detectives. It tests inks, food, water and athletes.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What does chromatography do?

It separates substances dissolved in a mixture. Colour writing!

Why do we draw the start line in pencil?

Pencil does not dissolve, so it does not run. Pencil stays Put.

Where must the solvent be, above or below the dots?

Below! Dots Above, Dip Below.

An ink makes three spots. How many dyes does it have?

Three! Count the spots, count the dyes.

On a machine graph, what does a tall peak show?

Lots of that substance! Peak height shows how much.
