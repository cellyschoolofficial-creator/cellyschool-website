Three big ideas

  1. About three quarters of the elements are metals.
  2. Metals and non-metals have different properties. But some elements break the rules.
  3. A mine only opens if the metal sells for more than it costs.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Name three properties of most metals.

Shiny, malleable, ductile, good conductors, high melting points. Metals Shine During Cold Hours!

Which three metals are magnetic?

Iron, nickel and cobalt.

What does brittle mean?

It breaks or shatters easily when hit.

Name a non-metal that conducts electricity.

Graphite!

What is an ore?

A rock with a metal compound in it. We get metal from it.
