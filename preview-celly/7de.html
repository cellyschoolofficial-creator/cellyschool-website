Memory tricks

Arrows point to the eater. Follow the energy.
Energy leaks at every link. Moving, keeping warm, and waste.
Primary means first. Secondary means second.
Poison piles up at the top.

Three big ideas

  1. Arrows show the direction energy flows. Arrows point to the eater.
  2. Energy leaks at every link. So there are usually fewer organisms further along.
  3. Persistent poisons pile up at the top. So pesticides must be used carefully.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Which way do the arrows in a food chain point?

The way energy flows. Arrows point to the eater.

Why do organisms respire?

To release energy, so they can move.

What is a trophic level?

A stage in a food chain. Like producer, or primary consumer.

Why are there fewer foxes than rabbits?

Energy is lost at each stage. So less energy reaches the foxes.

What does persistent mean?

It is not broken down in nature. It lasts a long time.
