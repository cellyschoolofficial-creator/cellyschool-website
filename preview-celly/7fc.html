Three big ideas

  1. The pH scale goes from zero to fourteen. Seven is neutral.
  2. Acid Down, Alkali Up, Seven in the Middle. Low number, Lots of acid.
  3. Universal indicator estimates pH by colour. A pH meter is more accurate.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What pH is neutral?

Seven! Seven in the Middle.

Which is more acidic, pH one or pH six?

pH one! Low number, Lots of acid.

Is pH twelve an acid or an alkali?

An alkali! It is above seven. Alkali Up.

Which is more accurate, universal indicator or a pH meter?

The pH meter! Indicator gives a quick estimate.

Why are pools kept at about pH seven point four?

To match the liquid in your eyes. And so chlorine can kill germs.
