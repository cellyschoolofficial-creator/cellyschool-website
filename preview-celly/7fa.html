Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

hazard

HazArd

anything that could cause harm

corrosive

CorRoSive

attacks metals, stonework and skin; can destroy living tissue

irritant

IrRiTant

makes skin sore and inflamed, but is unlikely to cause serious injury

dilute

DiLute

water has been added, making it less dangerous

concentrated

ConCenTratEd

very little or no water added

risk

Risk

the chance that a hazard will cause harm

precaution

PreCauTion

an action that reduces the risk

Three big ideas

  1. A hazard could cause harm. Six diamonds warn us which kind.
  2. Acids and alkalis can both be corrosive. Dilute is not always safe.
  3. The Hazard is the crocodile. The Risk is how close you get. The Precaution is the fence.

Quick quiz

What is a hazard?

Anything that could cause harm.

What does a corrosive substance do?

It attacks metals, stonework and skin. Corrosive Chews.

What is a risk?

The chance that a hazard will cause harm.

How should you smell a chemical?

Waft it! Waft, don't Whiff.

Is drain cleaner an acid or an alkali?

An alkali! And it is corrosive.
