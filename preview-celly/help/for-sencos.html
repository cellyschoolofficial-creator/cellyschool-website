Help for grown-ups

Dyslexia-friendly KS3 science resources for SENCOs and teachers

Celly School offers free KS3 science support for dyslexic pupils: every Year 7 Exploring Science lesson as a short-part video, with key words broken into syllables, memory tricks and a printable workbook with answers. It can be used for pre-teaching vocabulary, catch-up, homework and SEN support sessions.

Ways to use it in school

  1. Pre-teach vocabulary: share the key-word section of the next lesson before the topic starts.
  2. Catch-up and absence: pupils can watch the matching lesson at home, part by part.
  3. Intervention groups: one video part plus one workbook part fits a 20 to 30 minute session.
  4. Homework: the printable workbooks need little writing and include picture tasks, sorting and labelling.
  5. Parents: send families the lesson link so they can support at home.

How the materials are designed

Lexend font, cream backgrounds and short lines of text, in line with common dyslexia-friendly style advice.

One idea per part, with brain breaks and pause-and-recall questions.

Physics, chemistry and biology for all 12 Year 7 units, in the Exploring Science topic order.

Questions parents ask

Are the resources free for schools?

Yes. The videos and workbooks are free for home and classroom use. Please link to the website rather than uploading the files elsewhere.

Which scheme of work do the lessons follow?

The Year 7 Pearson Exploring Science topic order (units 7A to 7L). Celly School is independent and not affiliated with Pearson.

Are there resources for KS3 physics as well as biology and chemistry?

Yes. Units 7I Energy, 7J Current electricity, 7K Forces and 7L Sound are all covered with videos and workbooks.
