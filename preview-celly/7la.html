Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

vibrate

ViBrate

moving backwards and forwards, again and again

frequency

FreQuenCy

the number of vibrations each second

hertz

Hertz

the unit of frequency, Hz

amplitude

AmPliTude

the size of a vibration

Memory tricks

No wobble, no sound.
High and fast. Low and slow.
Big is loud. Small is soft.

Three big ideas

  1. Every sound is made by something vibrating.
  2. Faster vibrations mean a higher frequency, and a higher pitch.
  3. Bigger vibrations mean a bigger amplitude, and a louder sound.
