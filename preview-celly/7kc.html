Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

friction

FricTion

the force between two touching objects; it slows things or keeps them still

abseiling

AbSailIng

sliding down a rope, using friction to control your speed

lubricant

LuBriCant

something like oil or grease that reduces friction

resistance

ReSistAnce

friction from air or water that slows things down

Memory tricks

Friction fights the movement.
Oil the axles, never the brakes.
Grip, grind, glow.

Three big ideas

  1. Friction acts between touching objects. It slows things down or keeps them still.
  2. Friction can be useful, for grip. Or a problem, with wear and heat.
  3. Smooth surfaces and lubricants reduce friction.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is friction?

The force between two touching objects. It fights the movement.

Name two ways to reduce friction.

Make surfaces smooth. Or use a lubricant, like oil or grease.

Name two problems friction can cause.

It wears things away. It makes heat and noise.

Why does a drag racer open parachutes?

To increase air resistance, and slow down.
