Memory tricks

Evaporation at the Edge. Boiling Bubbles in the Body.
Yellow is for Yawning. Blue is for Busy.

Three big ideas

  1. Evaporation leaves dissolved solids behind. The solvent goes. The solute stays.
  2. Evaporation is at the surface, at any temperature. Boiling is all through, at the boiling point.
  3. Heat safely. A medium blue flame, eye protection, and turn off before it is dry.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is evaporation?

A liquid turning into a gas at its surface. Evaporation at the Edge.

Can water evaporate on a cold day?

Yes! Evaporation happens at any temperature. It is just faster when it is warmer.

What is the boiling point of pure water?

One hundred degrees Celsius! Boiling Bubbles in the Body.

Which flame is the safety flame?

The yellow flame! Yellow is for Yawning.

When you heat to dryness, when do you turn the burner off?

When most of the liquid has gone. Before it starts to spit.
