Key facts

What is an organism?

An organism is a living thing. You are an organism. A dog and a tree are organisms too.

What are the seven life processes?

Memory trick: Mrs Gren. Each letter starts one life process.

M – movement

R – reproduction

S – sensitivity

G – growth

R – respiration

E – excretion

N – nutrition

An organism does all seven, not just some of them.

Do plants move?

Yes, slowly. Most animals move their whole body from place to place. Plants stay in one place and move parts of themselves. Arctic poppy flowers turn to follow the Sun.

What is reproduction?

Making more living things like yourself. The young are called offspring. A cat has kittens. Many plants grow flowers, and the flowers make seeds.

What is respiration?

Respiration releases energy from food. It happens inside your cells, in tiny parts called mitochondria. Every organism respires, plants too.

Is respiration the same as breathing?

No. Breathing brings oxygen in. Respiration happens inside your cells. We get oxygen from the air with our lungs. Fish get oxygen from the water with their gills.

What are excretion and nutrition?

Excretion means getting rid of waste. Ex means out. When you breathe out, you get rid of waste gas.

Nutrition means getting the substances you need to live and grow. Animals eat food. Plants make their own food using light, and take small amounts of substances from the soil.

Is a car alive?

No. A car moves and uses fuel, a bit like food. But a car cannot grow and cannot reproduce. So a car is not an organism. A robot is not an organism either.
