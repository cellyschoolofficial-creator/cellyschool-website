Memory tricks

More Hits, More Push.
Vacuum means Very empty. No dancers at all.
Straws don't Suck. The air Shoves.

Three big ideas

  1. Air pressure is caused by air particles hitting a surface.
  2. More particles hitting an area means more pressure.
  3. A vacuum has no particles. The air outside does the pushing.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Which state can be compressed easily?

A gas! Gases Go everywhere.

How do particles move in a solid?

They vibrate on the spot. Solids Shake!

Soot specks dance under a microscope. Why?

Yes, Bolt! That is Brownian motion.

A smell spreads across a still room. What is it called?

Diffusion! Crowded to Clear.

Why is a can crushed when the air inside is pumped out?

Nothing pushes from inside. The outside air pressure crushes it.

What causes air pressure?

Air particles hitting a surface.

What is a vacuum?

A completely empty space. No particles, not even air.

How does a drink go up a straw?

Air pressure outside pushes it up. Straws don't Suck. The air Shoves.

Why does pumping air into a tyre raise its pressure?

More particles hit the tyre wall. More Hits, More Push.

Who studies the weather and makes forecasts?

A meteorologist!
