Metals and non-metals: free workbook

A printable workbook for the 7Hc lesson, made for children who find reading and spelling hard.

Download the workbook (PDF, 0.7 MB) Go to the 7Hc lesson

What is inside

  • Short parts that match the parts of the video
  • Picture activities, labelling and sorting
  • Key words with syllable taps, and memory tricks
  • Test-style questions, with answers for grown-ups
  • A QR code on each part that opens the matching part of the video

Printing tips

Print on cream or pale yellow paper if you have it; many dyslexic readers find it calmer than bright white. Print at 100% (actual size). You can print only the part you are working on today.

The workbooks are free to print and share for home and classroom use. Please don't sell them or upload them to other websites; share the link to this page instead.
