Meet the heroes

Celly the cell teaches biology, Atomy the atom teaches chemistry, and Volty the spark teaches physics. Bolt the robot joins every lesson and gets things wrong, so children can spot the mistake.

For parents, tutors and teachers

Celly School began at a kitchen table, when a Year 7 student understood the science but could not remember the words. The lessons use proven methods for teaching dyslexic learners (Orton-Gillingham style): multisensory steps, syllables, memory tricks and regular recall. Read more about how the lessons work.
