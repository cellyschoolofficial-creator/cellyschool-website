Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

solid

SolId

keeps its shape and volume; does not flow

liquid

LiqUid

flows and takes the shape of its container; keeps its volume

volume

VolUme

the amount of space something takes up, in cubic centimetres

compress

ComPress

squash into a smaller volume

density

DenSiTy

the mass of a certain volume of a material

Memory tricks

Shape, Space, Spill, Squash.
Dense means Crowded.

Three big ideas

  1. Solids keep their shape and volume. Liquids flow, but keep their volume.
  2. Gases spread out in all directions, and they are easy to compress.
  3. Density is mass for its size. Iron is denser than water.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What are the three states of matter?

Solid, liquid and gas.

Which state can be compressed easily?

A gas!

What is volume?

The amount of space something takes up.

What is density?

The mass of a certain volume. Dense means Crowded.

Does a liquid keep its shape?

No! It takes the shape of its container. But it keeps its volume.
