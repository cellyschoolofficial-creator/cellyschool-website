Help for grown-ups

Year 7 British-curriculum science help for families in Qatar and the Gulf

Many British-curriculum schools in Qatar and the Gulf teach Year 7 science with a KS3 scheme such as Exploring Science. Celly School offers free video lessons and printable workbooks for every Year 7 lesson, in that order, designed for children who find reading and spelling hard, including children learning in English as an additional language.

Why it helps families here

  1. English science vocabulary is often the hardest part, especially for children who speak Arabic or another language at home. Words are said slowly and broken into beats.
  2. Short video parts mean parents can support at home even if they studied science in another language.
  3. The workbooks use pictures, sorting and labelling, so children can show what they know with less writing.
  4. Lessons go out at 6 pm Qatar time, after school.

Getting started

Check which unit your child is on (for example 7A Cells or 7E Mixtures) and open that unit on our Year 7 page.

Watch one part together, tap the key words, then do the matching workbook part.

Questions parents ask

Does this follow the curriculum used in British schools in Qatar?

It follows the Year 7 Exploring Science topic order, which many British-curriculum schools use for KS3. Check your school's scheme of work to match units.

Is it free?

Yes. All videos and printable workbooks are free, with no sign-up.

Will there be Arabic support?

Arabic guides for parents are planned. The lessons themselves are in English, with every key word said slowly and broken into syllables.
