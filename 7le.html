Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

transverse

TransVerse

a wave where things move at right angles to the way the wave travels

longitudinal

LonGiTuDiNal

a wave where things move in the same direction as the wave travels

reflection

ReFlecTion

a wave bouncing back off a barrier

superposition

SuPerPoSiTion

when two waves meet, and add up or cancel out

Memory tricks

Ducks don't drift. They bob.
Longitudinal goes along. Transverse goes across.
Crest on crest makes a giant.

Practise

Three short games to help the words stick. Take your time: there is no timer.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Is sound a transverse or a longitudinal wave?

Longitudinal! Longitudinal goes along.

What happens when a crest meets a trough?

They cancel out. Crest on trough makes it flat.

Why do ripples get smaller as they spread out?

The energy spreads out more and more.
