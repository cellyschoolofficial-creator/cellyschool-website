Memory tricks

Every Alkali is a Base. But not every Base is an Alkali.
Antacid means Anti-acid.

Three big ideas

  1. A base neutralises an acid. An alkali is a base that dissolves.
  2. Neutralisation helps us every day. Antacids, toothpaste, rust removal and cleaner air.
  3. Never mix household chemicals. Never Mix, Never Risk.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a base?

A substance that neutralises an acid, making a salt and water.

What is an alkali?

A soluble base! Every Alkali is a Base.

How does an antacid help indigestion?

Its base neutralises some of the stomach acid. Gently, at about pH nine.

Why do many toothpastes contain a base?

To neutralise the acids in your mouth that cause tooth decay.

Should you ever mix household cleaners?

Never! Never Mix, Never Risk.
