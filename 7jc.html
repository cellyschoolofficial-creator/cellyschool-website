Memory tricks

Series is a single snake.
Parallel has paths.
Split and sum.
AND needs all. OR needs one.

Three big ideas

  1. A series circuit is one loop. One break, and everything stops.
  2. A parallel circuit has branches. The branch currents add up.
  3. More parallel branches keep bulbs bright, and the main current grows.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a series circuit?

A circuit with only one loop. A single snake!

What is a parallel circuit?

A circuit with branches that split apart and join up again.

The main current is zero point two amps. Two equal branches. Each one?

Zero point one amps each. Split and sum!

Two switches in series. Is it AND, or OR?

AND. Both switches must be closed.
