Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

hazard

HazArd

something that can cause harm

neutral

NeuTral

the blue wire, part of the circuit that makes an appliance work

fuse

Fuse

a wire that melts if too much current flows

circuit breaker

CirCuitBreakEr

a safety device that cuts off the current if it is too big

Memory tricks

Water and wires? Walk away.
Brown is busy. Blue is back. Green and yellow is ground.
Just above, not way above.

Practise

Three short games to help the words stick. Take your time: there is no timer.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Why is mains electricity more dangerous than a cell?

It has a much higher voltage.

What colour is the live wire?

Brown! Brown is busy.

What does a fuse do if the current is too big?

It melts, and breaks the circuit.

Name two electrical safety rules.

No wet hands. Nothing in sockets. No damaged wires. No overloaded sockets.
