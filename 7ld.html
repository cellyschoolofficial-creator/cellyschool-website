Key words

ACousTics

Memory tricks

Ultra means higher, not louder.
Bounce, soak or pass.
Send. Smack. Sense.

Three big ideas

  1. Sound can be transmitted, absorbed or reflected.
  2. An echo is a reflected sound. Its timing tells us distance.
  3. Ultrasound is too high to hear. We use it to clean, heal and find.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is an echo?

A reflected sound!

Name one way people use ultrasound.

Cleaning jewellery, treating injuries, or sonar to find fish.

Why do dolphins use sound, not light, to find food?

Sound travels through water much better than light.

What can happen when sound hits a material?

It can be transmitted, absorbed or reflected. Pass, soak or bounce!
