Three big ideas

  1. In a chemical reaction, atoms join with bonds to make new substances.
  2. A compound has different properties from the elements it is made from.
  3. Name a compound with the metal first, and an I. D. E. ending.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a compound?

A substance with atoms of different elements joined together.

How can you separate iron from an iron and sulfur mixture?

With a magnet!

What is a chemical reaction?

A change that makes new substances.

Magnesium and sulfur make what compound?

Magnesium sulfide! Metal goes First. I. D. E. goes at the End.

What is a mineral?

A compound found naturally in rocks.
