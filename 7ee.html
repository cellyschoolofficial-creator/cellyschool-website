Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

desalination

DeSalInAyTion

taking the salt out of salty water to make fresh drinking water

condense

ConDense

a gas cools and turns back into a liquid

distillation

DisTilLaTion

evaporate a liquid, then condense it again, to collect it

condenser

ConDenSer

the cold tube where steam condenses (Liebig condenser: a tube inside a tube)

distillate (◇)

DisTilLate

the liquid collected from distillation

Memory tricks

De means remove. Sal means salt. De-Sal means De-Salt!
Boil it, Cool it, Catch it.

Three big ideas

  1. Distillation separates a liquid from a mixture. Evaporate it, then condense it.
  2. Evaporate keeps the Solid. Distil keeps the Drink.
  3. Pure water boils at one hundred degrees. Distilled water is pure.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Why is sea water dangerous to drink?

Its salt pulls water out of your cells. It makes you thirstier.

What does desalination mean?

Taking the salt out of salty water. De-Sal means De-Salt.

What are the three steps of distillation?

Boil it, Cool it, Catch it! Evaporate, then condense.

Is the condenser hot or cold?

Cold! Cold water flows around it.

A liquid boils at one hundred degrees. Could it be pure water?

Yes! Pure water boils at one hundred degrees Celsius.
