Memory tricks

Models make predictions. Models have limits.
A good model behaves like the real thing.
Boiler is the battery. Radiator is the ray of light.

Three big ideas

  1. Models help us think about things we cannot see, and make predictions.
  2. A good model behaves like the real thing. Every model has limits.
  3. A current is a flow of charges. It carries energy.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What makes a model a good one?

It behaves like the real thing.

What is an electric current?

A flow of charges, carrying energy.

In the central heating model, what is like the bulb?

The radiator. Boiler is the battery. Radiator is the ray of light.

Why do metals conduct electricity?

Charges can move around easily inside them.
