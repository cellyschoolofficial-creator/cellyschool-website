Memory tricks

Inter is between. Intra is inside one species.
Say it with me. Continuous Can be any value. Discontinuous Drops into groups.
DRY MIX. Dependent, Responding, Y axis. Manipulated, Independent, X axis.

Three big ideas

  1. Variation means differences. There is more between species, and less within a species.
  2. Continuous Can be any value. Discontinuous Drops into groups.
  3. Independent on the x axis, dependent on the y axis.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a habitat?

The place where an organism lives.

Is foot length continuous, or discontinuous?

Continuous. But shoe size is discontinuous.

What is a species?

A group that can reproduce together, making offspring that can also reproduce.

Why can a zebroid not have babies?

It is a hybrid. Hybrids cannot reproduce.

Which axis does the independent variable go on?

The x axis, going across. DRY MIX.
