Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

eardrum

EarDrum

a thin skin inside the ear that vibrates when sound arrives

CochLeA

Three big ideas

  1. The eardrum vibrates. The tiny bones make the vibrations bigger.
  2. The cochlea makes impulses. You hear when they reach your brain.
  3. Loud sounds can damage your ears. Protect them.
