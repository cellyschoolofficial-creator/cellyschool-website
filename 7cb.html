Three big ideas

  1. The heart is a muscle that pumps blood. Arteries go Away. Veins come back.
  2. Red blood cells carry oxygen. White blood cells fight infections.
  3. Scientific questions can be tested. Ethical questions are about right and wrong.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

How do you work out your pulse rate?

Count the beats for fifteen seconds. Then multiply by four.

Which way do arteries carry blood?

Away from the heart.

Why do capillaries have thin walls?

So oxygen and nutrients can get out to the cells.

Why do red blood cells have no nucleus?

So there is more room for haemoglobin, to carry oxygen.

Is this question scientific or ethical? Should we test medicines on animals?

Ethical.
