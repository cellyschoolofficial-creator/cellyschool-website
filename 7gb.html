Key words

hypothesis

prediction

Three big ideas

  1. A hypothesis is tested with predictions. Lots of evidence makes a theory.
  2. All matter is made of tiny particles, always moving, held by forces.
  3. Solids Shake. Liquids Slide. Gases Go everywhere.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a hypothesis?

An idea about how or why something happens.

What makes a hypothesis into a theory?

Lots of supporting evidence!

What are the four points of the particle theory?

Tiny particles. Always moving. Forces of attraction. The forces change in each state.

How do particles move in a solid?

They vibrate on the spot. Solids Shake!

How do particles move in a gas?

Freely, in all directions. Gases Go everywhere!
