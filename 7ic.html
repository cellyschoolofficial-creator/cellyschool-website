Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

fuel

FuEl

a substance that stores chemical or nuclear energy that can easily be transferred

fossil

FosSil

the remains of something that lived long ago

non-renewable

NonReNewABle

will run out, because it can't be replaced as fast as we use it

biofuel

BiOFuEl

Memory tricks

Burning moves it, doesn't make it.
for the three fossil fuels. Cows Often Graze.
Coal from land plants. Oil from the ocean.
A fuel is a store. Electricity is a road.
Primary means you did it first. Secondary comes from someone else.

Three big ideas

  1. Fuels store energy. Burning transfers it, but doesn't make it.
  2. Fossil fuels formed over millions of years. They are non-renewable.
  3. Biofuels are renewable, because we can grow more.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is a fuel?

A substance that stores energy that can easily be transferred.

Name the three fossil fuels.

Coal, oil and natural gas. Cows Often Graze!

Why are fossil fuels non-renewable?

They take millions of years to form, so they will run out.

Is electricity a fuel?

No! A fuel is a store. Electricity is a road.
