Celly School makes free, calm, dyslexia-friendly science lessons for Year 7, in the Exploring Science topic order.

Why Celly School exists

It started with one Year 7 student preparing for a science test. She understood the ideas, but the long science words would not stay in her memory. The science videos online were not made for children like her, so her dad researched proven methods for teaching dyslexic children and began making the lessons himself, starting with Cells.

How the lessons work

  • Short parts. One idea at a time, with a brain break between parts.
  • Words in beats. Every new word is said slowly and tapped: one tap for each syllable.
  • Memory tricks. Silly sentences, gestures and pictures to hang each word on.
  • Pause and recall. Questions with thinking time, thumbs up or thumbs down, and a quiz at the end.
  • A friendly mistake-maker. Bolt the robot gets things wrong, so children practise spotting and fixing mistakes.
  • Free workbooks. Printable practice for home, with answers and QR codes back to the video.

Using the lessons at home

Watch one part, pause, and do the matching workbook part. Say the key words out loud together and tap the beats. Short and often works better than long sessions.
