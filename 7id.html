Three big ideas

  1. Renewable resources won't run out. But most depend on the weather.
  2. Nearly all our energy first came from the Sun.
  3. Only nuclear, tidal and geothermal don't depend on the Sun.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What is the difference between a solar panel and a solar cell?

Panels Pour hot water. Cells Create current!

Which two renewables are available at any time?

Hydroelectric and geothermal. Hydro and Geo go any time!

What does geothermal power use?

Hot rocks under the ground.

Name the three energy sources that don't depend on the Sun.

Tidal, geothermal and nuclear. Tides, Toasty rocks, Tiny atoms!
