Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

fracture

FracTure

A broken bone is called a fracture.

cartilage

CarTiLage

vertebrae

VerTeBrae

Your main support is your backbone. It is made of small bones called vertebrae.

tendon

TenDon

ligament

LigAMent

The bones in a joint are held together by ligaments.

Memory tricks

Tendons join muscle to bone. Ligaments link bone to bone.

Three big ideas

  1. Bones are living organs. They are strong, but light.
  2. The skeleton has three jobs. Support, protection and movement.
  3. Joints can be fixed, hinge, or ball and socket.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What are the three main jobs of the skeleton?

Support, protection and movement. Some People Move.

What does bone marrow make?

Blood cells.

What joins a muscle to a bone?

A tendon.

What holds the bones in a joint together?

Ligaments.

What kind of joint is your shoulder?

A ball and socket joint.
