Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

elastic

extension

proportional

ProPorTionAl

Memory tricks

Extension is extra.
Double the force, double the stretch.
Elastic escapes back. Plastic stays put.

Three big ideas

  1. Extension is the extra length when something stretches.
  2. For a spring, the extension is proportional to the force. Up to a limit.
  3. Elastic things spring back. Plastic things stay changed.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What does elastic mean?

It goes back to its original shape when the force is removed.

A spring goes from eight centimetres to twelve. What is the extension?

Four centimetres! Extension is extra.

Five newtons stretch a spring one centimetre. What about ten newtons?

Two centimetres! Double the force, double the stretch.

What is inside a force meter?

A spring!
