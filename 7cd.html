Three big ideas

  1. Muscles and bones form the locomotor system. Training makes both grow.
  2. Muscles only pull. So they work in antagonistic pairs, like biceps and triceps.
  3. Impulses from the brain make muscles contract. Mitochondria give them energy.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

Which two kinds of organ make the locomotor system?

Bones and muscles.

What happens to a muscle when it contracts?

It gets shorter and fatter, and it pulls on a bone.

Why do muscles work in antagonistic pairs?

Because muscles can only pull. They cannot push.

What carries impulses from the brain to a muscle?

The spinal cord, then nerves.

Where in a cell is energy released?

In the mitochondria.
