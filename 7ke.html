Key words

Say each word slowly and tap one beat for each part. Tapping the beats helps you spell and remember long science words.

balanced

BalAnced

two forces the same size, in opposite directions

unbalanced

impact

Memory tricks

Zero on the scoreboard, no change.
Balanced: boringly the same. Unbalanced: something changes.

Quick quiz

Say your answer out loud first, then check.

What are balanced forces?

Two forces the same size, in opposite directions.

What do balanced forces do to a moving object?

Nothing changes! It keeps going at the same speed.

A cyclist stops pedalling. Why does she slow down?

Friction and air resistance still pull back. The forces are unbalanced.

Why does a stretchy rope help a falling climber?

It makes the impact force smaller.
